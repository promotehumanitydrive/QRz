Privacy Policy for QR Toolkit

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Toolkit ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Summary

QR Toolkit is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server, and we do not require you to create an account to use the app.

Permissions We Use

  • Camera: Used only to scan QR codes in real time. Camera images are processed on your device to detect and decode QR content and are not saved, stored, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Photos / Storage: Used when you choose to scan a QR code from an existing photo, or when you save a QR code you've generated to your device. Files are only accessed or written when you explicitly choose to do so.

Sharing Feature

When you use the app's share function, you choose the destination (e.g. messaging apps, email) using your device's native share menu. We do not have access to what you share or with whom.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs that track your activity.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any in the first place.

Children's Privacy

QR Toolkit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data Security

Since all scanning and generation happens locally on your device, your QR code content never leaves your phone unless you actively choose to share it. We do not back up, sync, or upload any app data to cloud storage or any external server.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
